**Brand:** Acme Analytics, a unified analytics platform for growing B2B and e-commerce teams.

**Voice:** clear, practical and confident, never hype. We explain how things work and what a reader can do next. Short sentences. Plain words over jargon.

**Do:**
- Lead with the reader's problem or the single most useful takeaway.
- Use concrete numbers and examples **from the article only**.
- End LinkedIn posts with a question or a clear next step.

**Don't:**
- Use clickbait ("you won't believe"), exclamation marks in every sentence, or more than one emoji per post.
- Promise results the article doesn't support.
- Mention competitors by name.
